How a visit works

  1. Choose a slot

    Book online in under a minute. Slots run from 7 am to 7 pm, seven days a week.

  2. We park outside

    Your mechanic checks the bike, confirms the price with you, then gets to work. No surprise add-ons.

    Questions people ask

    Do you fix e-bikes?

    We service the mechanical parts of most e-bikes: brakes, gears, wheels and tyres. We don't open motors or batteries.

    What if you can't fix it on the spot?

    If a repair needs a part we don't carry, we order it and return within two days. You only pay the visit fee once.

    Where do you work?

    Anywhere we can park within reach of you: driveway, street, office car park. Enter your area when you book and we'll confirm coverage.

    Can I change or cancel my slot?

    Yes. Message us up to two hours before the slot and we'll move it or cancel it at no charge.
